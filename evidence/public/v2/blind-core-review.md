# Independent blind visual review

Reviewed only `.runtime/v2-validation-first-core/output/core_sheet.png`. I did not inspect the project, source, descriptions, or intended subject. This verdict concerns the displayed contact sheet, not playback at its original timing.

## What the silhouette suggests

The visible shape is a single pale, compact blob: approximately a rounded diamond or flattened teardrop, with a small point at the top, broad curved sides, and a slightly angled lower edge. A seed, pebble, or soft droplet is a plausible visual association. These are tentative associations, not a confident object identification. I cannot identify a character, animal, articulated body, or specific action from this silhouette alone.

## Continuity and construction

Each occupied frame shows one uninterrupted filled region. The perimeter is mostly smooth with small pixel steps. There are no visible holes, detached fragments, necks, joints, rings, chained spheres, or repeated capsules. It reads as one continuous mass. That continuity is clear, but the very simple outline provides few distinctive recognition cues.

## Form and motion readability

- **Apparition:** the first frame is empty; a small central shape grows into the full blob. The growth progression is easy to read, and the contour stays broadly consistent.
- **Stable:** the blob keeps roughly the same position and size. Small differences in width and contour suggest a mild wobble or pulse. There is no clear articulated movement, travel direction, or expressive pose.
- **Disparition:** the first six samples remain close to full size; the final sample is empty. At this sampling density, the disappearance reads as an abrupt cutoff. Any intermediate dissolve, shrink, or other exit motion is not legible here.

## Verdict

The sheet succeeds at showing a coherent, continuous blob and a clear appearance by growth. It does not establish a distinctive subject or a readable action beyond growth and slight deformation. The exit is poorly explained by the shown samples. No semantic match can be judged without knowing the intended subject; this review deliberately makes no such claim. Playback and denser sampling could change the assessment of timing and disappearance, but would not add the silhouette landmarks absent from these displayed frames.
